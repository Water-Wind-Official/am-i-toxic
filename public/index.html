A self-reflection quiz to identify potential toxic traits

Welcome!

This assessment presents realistic interpersonal dilemmas—situations where reasonable people might disagree about the right approach.

For each scenario, you'll rate several possible responses. There are no trick questions, but the "obvious" answer isn't always correct. Some responses that seem harsh are actually appropriate; some that seem kind are actually avoidant.

5 detailed scenarios with multiple responses each. Takes about 10 minutes. Rate each response 1-10 based on your honest judgment.
